Interpretation
--------------
The differential rows measure the complete fixed-key endpoint difference selected
from a best one-active-S-box characteristic. They do not observe whether that
specific internal characteristic was followed round by round.

The linear rows measure fixed-key endpoint correlation. They therefore include
linear-hull effects rather than measuring one internal trail in isolation.

The reduced-round endpoint measurements depart substantially from the selected
single-characteristic and single-trail products. These values are not a full-round
security assessment and do not establish or rule out a 16-round distinguisher.
